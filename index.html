How to Record

Short POV clips of everyday tasks. One page — read it once, then start.

Delivery deadline
Wednesday, 5 August 2026
The recording itself is about 30–40 minutes of work in total. If this date does not work for you, tell me now and we will agree on another one.

You only need to remember 3 things

  1. Phone on your head, tilted down
  2. Hands always in frame — face never in frame
  3. One task = one video = 20–30 seconds

Everything below is only detail for these three.

1. The angle

This is the part that decides whether a clip is usable. Put on the head strap and tilt the phone downward about 30–45° — roughly the angle you look at your own hands when working at a table.

Too highYou see the wall and the ceiling. Your hands are at the bottom edge or missing.
Too lowYou see mostly your feet or the floor right under you.
CorrectBoth hands and the object are clearly visible, near the middle of the frame.

The 10-second test — do this once before the real recording

  1. Put on the strap and stand where you will work.
  2. Record 5 seconds while you touch the object with both hands.
  3. Play it back and ask: can I see both hands and the object clearly?
  4. Yes → the angle is right. Keep it for all 4 videos.
    No → adjust the tilt and test again.

This test takes 10 seconds. Re-recording four clips takes 40 minutes. Please do the test.

3. Your 4 tasks

Your 4 assigned tasks are in the chat message I sent you. Each person gets a different set, so please use the list in your message, not someone else's.

For every video:

  • Start recording before you touch anything.
  • Do the task from beginning to end.
  • Stop recording after the task is finished.
  • 20–30 seconds. Not 5 seconds. Not 2 minutes.

If one of your tasks is not possible in your home, message me before you start and I will swap it.

5. Never record

  • Faces — yours or anyone else's
  • Other people
  • Documents, letters, screens, name tags, ID cards
  • Anything showing your address or personal details

If a face appears by accident, just record that clip again. It is not a problem.

6. Light

GoodBright enough that your hands and the object are clearly visible.
BadA bright window directly behind you — your hands turn into a dark shape.

If it looks dark on the screen, turn on a light or move to a brighter spot.

7. How to send

  1. Upload the 4 videos to Google Drive. (Dropbox, Mega or WeTransfer also fine. Drive is easiest — it is already on your phone and gives you 15 GB free.)
  2. Set the sharing to “anyone with the link can view”.
  3. Paste the link in our chat, and fill in the submission form I send you.

Please do not attach the videos in the chat directly — about 250 MB in total, it will fail.

Do not send through WhatsApp

WhatsApp re-compresses every video it sends. The resolution and the fine detail are destroyed and the clip becomes unusable for us — even though it still looks fine on your screen.

The same happens if Google Photos is set to “Storage saver” instead of “Original quality”.

Please upload the original file straight from your gallery or camera folder.

How to check: a 30-second 1080p / 30 fps clip should be roughly 50–80 MB. If your file is only 5–15 MB, it has been compressed somewhere — please upload the original instead.
